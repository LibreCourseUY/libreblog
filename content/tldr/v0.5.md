Here's what actually mattered in tech this week (September 14 to 21, 2026).

### Tech News

#### Linux

- **Linus Torvalds** released **Linux 7.3-rc4**, noting that code fixes continue to be driven heavily by LLMs and AI tools catching defects across the tree as reported by [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-7.3-rc4).
- Developers submitted patches to mainline upstream support for the fifteen-year-old **Amazon Kindle 4** running its modified Linux kernel, as detailed by [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Amazon-Kindle-4-Linux-Patches).
- A Google engineer published experimental patches introducing **Orphaned VMs**, allowing virtual machines to keep running uninterrupted while the host kernel goes offline for security updates via the Live Update Orchestrator, according to [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Orphaned-VMs-Linux-Patches).
- The Linux kernel's **perf subsystem** received a major overhaul replacing embedded Python and Perl with a standalone C-backed Python module for much faster trace scripting, covered by [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-Perf-Python-Module).

#### AI / Dev Tools

- **Cloudflare** announced that **Python Workers** are now generally available, enabling developers to run Python web frameworks and AI orchestration libraries natively in the edge runtime without writing JavaScript glue code, as announced on the [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/python-workers-ga/).
- **Cloudflare** detailed on their [Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/saving-100-tb-of-ram-with-math/) how they saved another 100TB of RAM across their Pingora-based services using statistical mathematics implemented in Rust.
- An **AI hallucination** regarding Chinese nuclear components nearly triggered a US military attack on a cargo ship, highlighting the risks of accelerating military AI adoption as reported by [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/report-us-almost-boarded-chinese-ship-over-hallucinated-ai-arms-report/).
- **Amazon** rejected **Meta's Muse AI shopping agent** over credentials and transparency concerns while trying to protect its customer journey, according to [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/21/amazon-shows-metas-muse-ai-shopping-agent-the-door/5297777).

#### Hardware & Devices

- **Apple** launched its new **M6 Mac mini**, which reviewers praised for its raw power while sharply criticizing a steep $300 price hike that ruins its previous value status, as reviewed by [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apple-m6-mac-mini-review-300-price-hike-spoils-a-nice-upgrade/).
- Apple sent out the **M5 Ultra Mac Studio** featuring an outrageous 36-core CPU, 80-core GPU, and 256GB of RAM geared specifically toward heavy AI workloads, tested by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/998040/apple-mac-studio-m5-ultra-initial-impressions-testing-review).
- Google and its hardware partners officially launched the **Googlebook** laptop lineup starting at $899, aiming to deeply integrate Android phones with traditional PC hardware starting October 4th, as broken down by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/997659/googlebooks-breakdown-acer-asus-hp-lenovo-dell).
- **Raspberry Pi** sparked controversy among hardware modders by blocking users from changing RAM chips on new boards, according to discussions on the [Raspberry Pi Forums](https://forums.raspberrypi.com/viewtopic.php?p=2380887#p2380888).

#### Security & Platforms

- Ransomware group **Clop** got a taste of its own medicine after a rival crew, **ShinyHunters**, hijacked their extortion leak site and threatened to expose companies that paid to keep quiet, reported by [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/21/clop-gets-a-taste-of-its-own-medicine-after-shinyhunters-hijack-leak-site/5297702).
- Eligible US iPhone owners who purchased an iPhone 15 Pro or any iPhone 16 model can now submit claims for a payout following Apple's **$250 million Siri AI lawsuit settlement** over delayed upgrade features, covered by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/998191/apple-siri-ai-iphone-16-class-action-lawsuit-settlement).
